# Xenon — Egyptian Search Keyword Map

Keyword research for the Egyptian fire-safety / security market (AR + EN), grouped by
search intent and mapped to target pages. Volume tiers are **qualitative** — inferred
from Egyptian directory listing counts (Yellow Pages: ~695 "مكافحة حريق" companies
nationally, 365 in Cairo, 102 in Alexandria) and from terms competitors actively rank
on. Validate exact volumes in Google Keyword Planner / Ahrefs with **geo = Egypt** and
**language = Arabic** before committing budget.

> Note on the `<meta keywords>` tag (in `messages/*.json → keywords.list`): Google
> ignores it. It carries minor weight only on Bing/Yandex and is useful as an internal
> reference. Real ranking lift comes from **titles, descriptions, H1/H2, body copy,
> image alt text, and structured data** — which is where the terms below should live.

---

## 1. Head terms (highest volume — own these)

The category term Egyptians and directories actually use is **مكافحة حريق**, not the
literal "السلامة من الحرائق". Prioritise it in H1s and titles.

| انظمة اطفاء حريق | fire fighting systems Egypt | Home / Services |

| طفايات حريق | fire extinguishers Egypt | Services |

## 2. Transactional intent (ready to buy / hire — convert these)

(supply / install / maintain) and **price** prefixes (سعر / اسعار).

## 6. Geo modifiers (append to head terms)

Egyptians frequently search by city **and district**. Build location relevance in
Projects copy and (recommended) dedicated location landing pages.

`القاهرة · الجيزة · الاسكندرية · مدينة نصر · مصر الجديدة · 6 اكتوبر · العاصمة الإدارية الجديدة · العين السخنة`

## 7. Sector verticals (append to head terms / use in Projects)

`مصانع (factories) · مستشفيات (hospitals) · مولات (malls) · بنوك (banks) · فنادق (hotels) · مستودعات/مخازن (warehouses) · مدارس وجامعات (schools)`

## 8. Informational — blog content opportunities (top-of-funnel)

Low competition, high authority-building value. Each is a strong blog/article topic
that captures research-stage traffic and internally links to Services.

- ما هو الفرق بين نظام الانذار المعنون والتقليدي؟ (addressable vs conventional)
- اشتراطات الدفاع المدني للمنشآت / الكود المصري للحريق كود 305
- مكونات نظام انذار الحريق
- متى تحتاج نظام اطفاء FM200 ومتى CO2؟
- جدول صيانة طفايات الحريق ومواعيد اعادة التعبئة
- اسعار انظمة مكافحة الحريق في مصر (price guide)

## Page → keyword priority summary

| Page | Lead keywords |
|---|---|
| **Home** | مكافحة حريق · fire fighting company Egypt · انظمة اطفاء وانذار حريق |
| **Services** | per-service head + transactional (توريد وتركيب وصيانة, سعر) |
| **Projects** | sector + geo (مصانع/مستشفيات/مولات + القاهرة/الجيزة) |
| **About** | trust/compliance (الدفاع المدني, الكود المصري, UL/FM/EN54) |
| **Contact** | transactional (اسعار, عرض سعر, توريد وتركيب وصيانة) |
| **Partners** | brand names + معتمد/authorized distributor |
| **Blog (recommended)** | informational cluster (section 8) |

## Recommended next steps (beyond metadata)

1. **Add an Arabic blog** targeting section 8 — biggest untapped organic opportunity.
2. **Location landing pages** (مكافحة حريق القاهرة / الجيزة / الاسكندرية) for local pack.
3. **Weave head terms into visible H1/H2 and service body copy**, not just meta.
4. **Image alt text** on `/public/services/*` using AR+EN service names.
5. **Google Business Profile** in Arabic — critical for "مكافحة حريق + [district]" local results.
6. Validate volumes in Keyword Planner (geo: Egypt, lang: Arabic) and prune low-value terms.
